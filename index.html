The Estate

A private mansion, warmly reawakened.

Behind carved sandstone gates, Raja Kothi unfolds in cool colonnaded courtyards and frescoed halls, restored by hand so heritage is something you live inside — not merely look at.

We keep it intimate. Fewer rooms, more attention: a butler who knows your name, a kitchen that cooks to your mood, and evenings under a sky full of stars.
