The Estate

A private mansion, reawakened for the modern traveller.

Behind carved sandstone gates, Raja Kothi unfolds as it always has — cool colonnaded courtyards, frescoed halls and gardens scented with jasmine. Every corner has been restored by hand, so that heritage is something you live inside, not merely look at.

We keep the estate deliberately intimate. Fewer rooms, more attention. A butler who knows your name, a kitchen that cooks to your mood, and evenings that end under a sky full of stars.
